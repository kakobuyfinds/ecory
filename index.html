What to check before you order

Match the variant and measurements

Open the listing and confirm the color and size tied to the displayed price. Compare garment measurements with a piece you own. For shoes, look for the insole length in centimeters.

Review QC photos

If your shopping agent provides quality-control photos, compare the received item with the selected variant. Check the seams, labels, closures and visible damage. Request a measurement or close-up if a detail is unclear.

Check shipping and returns

Check whether the shipping quote uses actual or volumetric weight. Confirm the seller’s return window and the agent’s return process while the item is still at the warehouse.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

Each item links to its HipobuyFind listing. The two main buttons open the HipobuyFind homepage. Check your chosen agent’s ordering page for source-link compatibility.

Do the listed prices include shipping?

The table shows reference item prices. Use your own quotes for domestic delivery, international shipping, agent fees and applicable taxes.

How do I find a listing again?

Search for its name or the numeric ID at the end of the product URL. Use Save to keep the listing in your shortlist in this browser.
